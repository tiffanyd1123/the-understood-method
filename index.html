The Course

Seven steps. Start to resolution.

01

Get the full picture first

Before you ever talk to the customer, get the reason for the escalation from the last agent. Keep it simple. Repeat it back so nothing gets lost in the handoff.

02

Ask permission to hear it again

Introduce yourself, mention what you were told, then ask if it's okay to hear their side directly — "in case anything was missed." This one line is where most of the effect comes from.

03

Let them vent, take notes

Most angry callers aren't angry about the issue — they're angry about feeling unheard. Give them the floor. Write down what matters while they talk.

04

Show you're in their corner

Empathize. Sympathize. Confirm they've got the right person and that you're going to try to help — not just process them.

05

Set expectations early

Explain what you need to do next, and be upfront that a resolution isn't guaranteed. Under-promising here builds trust for whatever comes next.

06

Deliver it straight

Good news or bad, say it clearly and explain the reasoning. No runaround, no corporate hedging.

07

Follow through

If it's not resolved today, own it. Be the one they can actually reach — that's what turns a bad call into a saved customer.

That's it.

No fluff, no filler modules. Just the system, taught the way it's actually used on a live call.
